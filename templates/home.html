About DeepFake Detection

Deepfake technology uses artificial intelligence and deep learning techniques to manipulate digital media. It can be used to create realistic-looking videos in which a person's facial appearance has been altered or replaced.

Our DeepFake Detection system analyzes video frames and detects faces before using an Xception-based deep learning model to classify the video as either real or fake. The system also provides a confidence score with the prediction.

The application is designed to provide a simple way for users to upload a video, perform automated analysis, and receive a detection result without permanently storing the uploaded video.

Our Detection Technology

The system combines video frame processing, face detection, and deep learning to analyze uploaded videos.

Selected video frames are processed, faces are detected, and the resulting face images are passed to the Xception-based detection model. The predictions are aggregated to produce the final result.

For Business

Deepfakes can be used to impersonate individuals, manipulate their appearance, and spread misleading or fabricated media.

A deepfake detection system can provide an initial indication of whether a video may contain manipulated facial content. Such results should be considered alongside other evidence when authenticity is important.

Businesses can face risks from manipulated media, impersonation, misinformation, and fraudulent communications involving synthetic or altered content.

Automated deepfake detection can serve as an additional verification step when reviewing potentially manipulated video content.
